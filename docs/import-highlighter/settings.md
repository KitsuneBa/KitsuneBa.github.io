# Customization & Controls

The **Import Highlighter** window includes built-in controls to manage tracked items and customize colors and fade durations.

---

## 🎛️ Window Controls

* **Item List:**
  * `📁` **Folders** and `📄` **Files** are listed in order, colored according to whether they were added/imported or deleted.
  * Clicking an active item selects and pings it in the Project window (`●`).
  * Deleted items are marked with `✕` so you can verify what was removed.
* **Footer Status Bar:**
  * Displays live counters for **Add** (imported/moved items) and **Del** (deleted items).
  * **Clear:** Immediately clears all active highlights and resets the list.
  * **Settings:** Expands or collapses the settings panel at the bottom of the window.
  * **Hide:** Closes the window and prevents it from auto-opening on future imports until you manually open it again via **Tools &rarr; 🦊KitsuneBa's Tools🦊 &rarr; 🦊Import Highlighter🦊**.

---

## ⚙️ Customizing Settings

Click the **Settings** button in the footer to customize:

* **Time (Fade Duration):**
  * Use the `◀` and `►` buttons to decrease or increase how many seconds highlights remain visible before fading out.
* **Add Color:**
  * Color picker for newly imported or moved assets (default: soft purple `#9966FF`).
* **Del Color:**
  * Color picker for deleted assets (default: soft red `#FF6666`).
* **Reset (`R`):**
  * Click the `R` button next to the color pickers to restore the default highlight duration and colors.
